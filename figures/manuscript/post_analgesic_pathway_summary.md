# Post-analgesic pathway analysis (sensitivity)

Time zero = first analgesic after initial pain documentation. Event = first pain score documented strictly after analgesic. Censor at ED departure.

- Primary subgroup: trauma + pain=10 (N=2,167)
- Secondary: all trauma with analgesic (N=12,066)
- Full analgesic cohort (N=12,659)

**Interpretation:** This is a pathway/sensitivity analysis. It does not replace the primary initial-pain-to-reassessment analysis. Attenuation of demographic associations after analgesia suggests signals may be upstream of treatment or early clinical attention.
